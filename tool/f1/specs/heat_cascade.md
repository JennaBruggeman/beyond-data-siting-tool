## 1. Program and rooms

The cascade = the data centre + a chain of heat users, each taking water the stage above has cooled (Lund 2005, typ_heat_cascade_lund2005): **spa (water 36–40 °C) → pool (24–29 °C) or greenhouse (bench 35–40 °C) → heated public edge (fluid 17–19 °C, light snow)**. The **core = the heat-recovery room + the heated public edge + the spa** (proposed, Part 8 q1): the spa is the hottest stage and the smallest wet room, the edge is the coolest and needs no floor, and the heat-recovery room is the hand-over. The pool drops first (largest floor, rezoning in Vancouver, variance in Edmonton), the greenhouse second.

Rank: 1 never drops; higher numbers drop first. Fits shrink rooms toward their minimum before a room drops by rank. Sharing between spa and pool (desk, change, universal room, staff, chemical/laundry/custodial) follows Eastglen, where the hot pool and steam room share the lap pool's support (arch_sup_eastglenRooms).
