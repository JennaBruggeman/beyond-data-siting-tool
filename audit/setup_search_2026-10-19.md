# Set-ups that open the most opportunities (19 Oct 2026)

J.B.: find the set-up with the most opportunities, then second and third. Chosen: top 3 by count, shown as counts (changes the no-scores rule for this one card; the card says counts treat every opportunity and gate the same).

## How it works
- Tries every combination of cooling inside (3), how heat leaves (3), plant position (3), backup power (3), size (typical 2.5 MW / in between / largest that fits): 243 set-ups. Other answers kept; the user's answers restored after.
- Each set-up runs the 15 opportunity cards, 6 gates each (90 gates).
- Ranked by whole opportunities possible, then gates passing, then gates passing with a change, then fewest failing; ties go to the set-up with fewest changes from the user's.
- Each shown set-up lists the gates it improves or worsens against the user's, and what its changed choices give up (from the set-up cards' own downsides, not counted).
- 1.5–5.5 s per lot (headless).
