## The join
- BCA roll number: 15 digits. City folio: 12. The last 12 digits of the roll are the folio.
- Matched: 99.4% of non-residential rows, 99.96% of residential rows (jurisdiction 200).
- Matching on fewer digits joins the wrong lot, so it is not tried.

## Strata
- Non-residential: each strata lot's row repeats the whole building's gross area. Counted once per parcel (same area, basement, year).
- Field 21 (strata lot area): the guide calls it the development total; in the data the rows add up to about one building. Not used.
- Residential: one row per home. Strata lot areas × 1.15 for the size test (BCA's commercial-guide common-area allowance, borrowed).

## Result
- 91,159 Vancouver parcels with a heat estimate; 1,022 over the size thresholds (Edmonton ~1,050); 699 added (others within 40 m of a measured building or another receiver).
- Check against 238 measured buildings: estimate ÷ measured median 1.74, quartiles 1.0 and 3.2.
- Shown as a range in both cities: estimate ÷ 1.7 to the estimate. Edmonton could not be checked (no measured data published).
