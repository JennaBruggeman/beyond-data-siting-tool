2    2.2.1          Seismic Concerns
3    Most of the Murrin Substation site is located on seismically unstable ground with
4    eight underground transmission circuits traversing seismically unstable soil. 2 These
5    transmission circuits include the four 60 kV transmission lines that radially supply
6    Dal Grauer Substation. In a large seismic event, Murrin Substation and these
7    underground transmission circuits could be damaged and up to two thirds of
8    downtown customers, including all customers served by Dal Grauer Substation,
9    could experience a lengthy outage. It could take several weeks to restore power to
10   customers and years to completely restore the system from the damage depending
11   on the magnitude of the seismic event.

12   2.2.2          Lack of Capacity for Load Growth
13   The existing substations in the Downtown Vancouver Electricity Supply System are
14   at their final stage of development with no room to add more capacity. New 12 kV
15   feeder positions are required to supply new customers on the existing 12 kV manual
16   dual-radial system; however, by fiscal 2027, all the 12 kV feeder positions in the
17   Study Area will be in use, and BC Hydro will not have any feeder positions available
18   to connect new customers and meet future load growth. To connect new customers
19   in the interim period, BC Hydro will implement temporary measures such as loading
20   the existing feeders above normal planning load guidelines or connecting new
21   customers to available transfer-tie positions in other stations.

12   conversion cannot be realized.
