system operatorAESO, Large Load Integration Phase 2A — Proposed BYOG Process (26 Jun 2026, proposal, not final). Applies to transmission-connected loads asking for 75 MW or more of demand transmission service (DTS), site-aggregated; excludes loads under 75 MW, distribution-connected load and storage. First intake gas-fired generation only, "net new"; tethering ratio: generator output at 25 °C, 40% humidity equals DTS contract capacity. Data centres must show municipal zoning or area-structure-plan approval and accept the data-centre connection requirements. DTS commitment fee $15k/MW (refundable). Bridging before the generator runs: at most 20/30/40% of the DTS request by generator milestone, 1,600 MW across all intakes, up to 3 years; bridging load is fully interruptible, reduced before all other non-firm load to avoid a shortfall, curtailment scheme TBD (pro rata, rotation or combination); suspended for failing to curtail source
system operatorAESO, Large Load Integration Phase 2A BYOG Process Update, stakeholder session deck (18 Jun 2026, updated 24 Jun 2026). About 20 GW of data-centre load requested each quarter since Q2 2025 (19.2 GW Q2 2026). Phase 2 is modular: 2A BYOG, 2B ISO tariff, 2C "Grid Partners" (flexible load structures, operating procedures, market products), not yet set. Load management: data centres prioritized above other loads for reduction under supply-related conditions; in standard operation, if firm load shed is needed in an emergency, data centres reduce before all other firm loads; procedures (pro rata, blocks, rotations) to be developed. 74.9 MW applications rose from 9 (Jun 2025) to 18 (Jun 2026) in the 25–75 MW band; AESO may lower the 75 MW threshold.
