Lot area: 5,845 m² (City parcel data).
Zone floor-space ratio 3: 5,845 × 3 = 17,535 m².
Storeys under the 18.3 m height limit: first floor 6.0 m, then data-hall floors of 6.0 or 5.5 m. (18.3 − 6.0) ÷ 6.0 = 2.05, whole floors 2, plus the first floor = 3; with 5.5 m floors, 2.24 → 2 + 1 = 3.
Footprint from the site setup: the most the zone allows, less setbacks, loading and the plant yard: 4,356 m² (drawn in 3D).
Most floor area those storeys can hold: 4,356 m² × 3 = 13,068 m². The smaller of this and step 2: 13,068 m².
Ground floor (no data storage at grade): floor area ÷ storeys = 13,068 ÷ 3 = 4,356 m². Floors above: 13,068 − 4,356 = 8,712 m².
Data hall, strict reading (only the floors above, and only part of them is hall): 8,712 × 0.48 = 4,182 m², up to 8,712 × 0.72 = 6,273 m².
Data hall, lenient reading (support rooms on the ground floor, so the hall share applies to the whole building, but the hall still fits only on the floors above): smaller of 13,068 × 0.48 and 8,712 = 6,273 m², up to smaller of 13,068 × 0.72 and 8,712 = 8,712 m².
Servers: the smallest hall laid out loosely, 4,182 ÷ 630 m² per MW = 6.64 MW; the largest hall laid out densely, 8,712 ÷ 290 = 30.0 MW.
