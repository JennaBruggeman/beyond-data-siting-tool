# Step 8 sweep, 20 Oct 2026

Two parts:

- **Screens:** every step of the three worked examples in each template (resident, community, full), 9 walks. A screenshot and the panel text were taken at every step, including the panel text with "All the detail" opened. Three reviewers (Claude subagents) each read one example; Claude then checked each finding against the code before fixing it.
- **Lots:** 1,010 sampled lots, each walked as a user would. Finished 20 Oct, about 4 pm; the findings are in sensitivity_2026-10-20.md, "Run on 1,010 sampled lots".

## Fixed

1. **Size headline.** "Largest data centre that fits" showed the floor-space-only range (e.g. 4.9–22 MW). It now leads with the size that fits with its plant, generators and loading (10.8 MW); the floor-only range follows. On the set-up screen the range is now labelled "on floor space alone".
2. **Default set-ups that fit no size.** On many small lots the defaults fit nothing, and the user was left with only "No size fits with these choices". A new "Find a set-up that fits" button runs the same placement search the worked examples use. If nothing fits, it says "No set-up fits this lot".
3. **Dry coolers vs air-cooled chillers.** Air-cooled halls are laid out with air-cooled chillers (plant.js, M31), but several texts said "dry coolers". Every user-facing mention now names the plant actually laid out (`BD.dryName()`). This covers the residents' card, the cooling bar, water, Over time, the report and the comparisons.
4. **What the heat is worth.**
   - The report subtracted the heat pump's electricity from the heat value, but each cost per MWh already includes it (cards.js lcoh). This double-counted the electricity.
   - The report now uses the same figure as the heat step: the gas a MWh replaces (Vancouver about $40, Edmonton about $13).
   - The heat step's "What the heat is worth here" now gives both figures: the gas replaced, and what is left once the heat pump's electricity is paid.
   - **The Clark Dr and 123 Street PDFs sent earlier today show the wrong values.**
5. **Homes' worth of heat.** Two pages (comparisons, Two cities) divided by a home's bought energy; the rest of the tool uses the heat it needs (× 0.85, logic test 19 Oct). The rule is now the same everywhere.
6. **Report, floor per MW.** The report divided the zone's whole floor by the size; it now uses the floor the servers need (the 3D label's figure).
7. **Report, building counts.** The cover counted every building within 3 km (729); it now counts those with a known heat demand (688) and notes the rest. The "By type" list no longer drops types.
8. **Three options.** When the chosen size is the largest that fits, options B and C were identical. B is now that size air-cooled, and C is that size with heat to neighbours.
9. **Report, page references** ("see page 6") were wrong in the residents' report. They now name the section, and only when that section is in the report.
10. **Report, ruled-out rows** ("Backup power … not here") now give the test that fails, not an unknown.
11. **Report, existing towers.** The report drew only the leased floors at 555 W Hastings. The building on the lot is now drawn at its measured height, and the key figure gives that height (162 m).
12. **"N more here" box.** It listed some names twice, cut one name to "Offer", and listed the opportunity already open on the page. Names now appear once and in full, and the open one stays shown.
13. **Empty steps.**
    - The residents' Real buildings step had every section hidden, so it showed an empty page; residents now skip it.
    - A step with a single section (Set-ups that open the most) showed only its closed row; that section now opens.
14. **Sight lines in the residents' template** stayed on "Working out sight lines…": the panel was redrawn before the result arrived. The result now fills every copy and is kept for redraws.
15. **Wording.**
    - "All 9 heat opportunities are not ruled out here" became "None of the 9 heat opportunities is ruled out here".
    - "It is not possible here" now names the opportunity.
    - "3 possible here, 2 possible with a change" (counts of tests) became "3 tests pass, 2 pass with a change".
    - The residents' set-up row now reads "Air-cooled chillers" (the long version wrapped one word per line).
    - The stray space in "$161 –$220" is gone.
16. **Server refreshes.** 25 years at every 5–6 years is 4–5 refreshes, not 4; the embodied-carbon range now uses 4–5. The report's "8–9 sets" became "9–10 sets over 50 years".
17. **Edmonton site, Vancouver wording.** Three unknowns and one reason were Vancouver-only but read as general. They now say which city they are about:
    - the saltwater fire system;
    - detention credit for a harvesting tank;
    - Metro Vancouver's dispersion modelling for biomass;
    - the Alberta roof-lease question.
18. **Water hardness.** The Edmonton hardness note appeared on Vancouver sites with no cooling towers; it now shows only with towers.

## Open: for J.B. to decide

- **Plain words for residents.** The residents' template still shows engineering terms: PUE, N+1, dBA as sound power, COP, kWp, CWEC, 12 kV feeder, "Virginia ratio, transferred", "register's confidence". Options: plain-language names with a "?" for the term, or move those rows up a template.
- **Visible working notes.** Notes such as "(sources/15d §5)", "to check", "(NFPA standards not read)" and "not yet sourced" are visible to users. Your rule is to mark anything unverified. Proposal: keep the mark but use one plain form, "not yet verified".
- **"How it compares" rows.** On the Heat, Electricity, Water and Materials steps the row only names the comparisons ("the same servers, conventional here, at two real data centres…") with no figures until opened. When the set-up is already conventional, "This design" and "Conventional" are the same row.
- **Evidence labels that disagree with the unknowns.** "Greenhouse" is tagged pilot and "Aquaculture" built elsewhere, yet both list "not yet known: a built precedent". Cooling for neighbours is "not applicable" on the Electricity step but "not ruled out" under People. These are register entries to reconcile.
- **Zone DD (downtown) is "Not yet reviewed".** Its envelope gives about 233,600 m² of floor on a 6,244 m² lot (FSR about 37), which is not plausible. The DD schedule needs reading.
- **Edmonton gaps users see.** The tool has no non-market housing list for Edmonton, and three rules are "not yet known" in Edmonton but answered for Vancouver (district energy utility rule, condensate, public EV charging). These go to the research to-do list.
- **The 3D view.**
  - Roof plant is drawn at grade by design ("shown at grade"), and labels overlap the units.
  - On steps without 3D (Real buildings, Over time, Two cities, Report) the panel changes width.
- **Smaller items.**
  - The Edmonton electricity price is given as a $43.68 pool average on one page and $50 energy charges on another; the $50 includes transmission and operating reserve, but the page doesn't say so.
  - The noise limit appears in two forms: "55 dBA … quiet-zone home" and "45–55 dBA by zone".
  - "Plant seen from 30 of 60 nearby homes" doesn't say which 60.
  - The Real buildings heading says "10 buildings", but the table lists 8.
  - The stage name "Characterise" uses -ise; Canadian spelling is usually -ize.
