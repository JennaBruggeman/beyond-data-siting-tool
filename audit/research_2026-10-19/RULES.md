# Research rules (J.B., binding)
- Do not make things up or assume. Every figure needs a source you actually read; quote the exact words with page/section.
- No popular sources (news, blogs, Wikipedia, directories, SEO sites). Use government, regulator, statute/bylaw, standards bodies, utilities' regulatory filings, peer-reviewed papers, government labs. Trade press only if nothing else exists, labelled "trade press".
- Operator, manufacturer and consultant figures are CLAIMS: label them.
- Do not circumvent blocked sites (no curl, no mirrors, no archives). If WebFetch fails twice, record the URL under "Could not read".
- Do not download files. Do not edit anything outside your own output file.
- Canadian spelling. Short lines, no long paragraphs.
- Security (revised by J.B., 19 Oct 2026: "all of this is public information"): substations and other utility infrastructure may be shown and used where a government or utility source publishes them; permits and spec sheets that name generators and fuel are used in full (counts, sizes, fuel volumes, emission limits, test hours). One line kept by Claude, open for J.B. to overrule: generator and fuel-tank positions at real, named data centres are not drawn on the map. The working line from 11 Oct stands: nothing that works as a guide to defeating security (blind spots, coverage gaps, entry routes, staffing times).
- Parity: research Vancouver AND Edmonton to the same depth; say plainly where one city has no equivalent.
- Confidence scale (tool): 100 arithmetic/physics · 90 regulation/tariff/standard read directly · 80 government dataset or statistic · 70 peer-reviewed coefficient transferred · 60 derived with stated assumption · 50 operator or industry claim · 40 proxy · 20 judged · 0 gap.
## Output file format
For each topic: What the tool could compute or show (1–3 lines) · Findings (bullets: claim — exact quote — source, date, URL, source type, confidence) · Vancouver vs Edmonton · Proposed node(s) or change (id, formula or rule, inputs, which tier: resident/community/full) · Could not read / still a gap.
