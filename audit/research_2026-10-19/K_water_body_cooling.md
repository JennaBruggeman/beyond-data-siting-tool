### Is it cold enough? (DERIVED — engineering check needed)
- **Burrard Inlet (deep, Outer Harbour):** bottom ≤ 10.9 °C all year → well below 18–27 °C air supply and 24–45 °C liquid loops. Free cooling year-round looks feasible on temperature alone. Central Harbour bottom reaches 13.9 °C in summer — still adequate.
- **Fraser North Arm:** summer river likely ~18–20 °C (Qualark proxy). Marginal for air-cooled halls in August; fine for warm-water liquid cooling. North Arm figures UNVERIFIED.
- **North Saskatchewan:** ~0 °C for ice season → ample cold; frazil/anchor ice at intakes is the risk, not warmth. Summer values UNVERIFIED.
- Flow needed, seawater (DERIVED: Q = P / (ρ·c·ΔT), ρc ≈ 4.09 MJ/m³·K): ≈ **49 L/s per MW at ΔT 5 K; 31 L/s per MW at ΔT 8 K (Enwave's 4.4→12.5 °C); 24 L/s per MW at ΔT 10 K.**
