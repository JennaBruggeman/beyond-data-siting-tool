## Weaknesses first
- "Live" here is the tool's own part list, not a screen-by-screen read. 137 parts have **no card in any map**: they run, show in the logic view, and may feed figures elsewhere, but where each number appears on screen is not mapped. They are listed with "(no card mapped)"; a second column names the tool code files that mention the part (source notes, register ids), which points to where it is used but is not proof that a number is shown. 95 parts are named by no card map and no code file: likely shown only in the logic view.
- The maps cover the Engineering cards, three Architecture site cards and the Municipal cards; the other Architecture cards (typology, checks, visuals) light no parts yet, so their figures are not attributed here.
- Trust is the part's own rating; CLAUDE.md says a part's trust is the lowest of its inputs, which logic.json does not apply here (upstream inputs are listed in the logic view).
- **65 parts have q < 60** (flagged ⚑ below, and listed first).

## The two questions
**(1) distribution_cost.** Method: CAD 1,038–2,620 per metre of trench for DN100, from Persson et al. 2019 (EU, 2015 €) and npro.energy (EU catalogue prices), × 1.5782 CAD/€. **No Canadian or Vancouver per-metre pipe cost is in the register.** Canadian sources held are project totals with no length: Vancouver NEU expansion 2018 ("$13 to $37 million", register vanNEU2018: "cannot yield a $/m rate"), Edmonton DDE 2025–26 rate filing (Phase 1 $35.9M lumped with the energy centre and transfer stations; edmDDEcapital: "no pipe length or per-metre cost"). Squamish NEU feasibility 2010 gives levelised costs, not $/m. Cambridge MA Appendix 5 ($/ft, US) is the nearest North American per-length figure.

**(2) Loop temperature.** Two values are in use:
- **37.5 °C** (supply from the servers, 24 °C return; NREL ESIF, Van Geet 2019; cards.js `HS.src`): the **Engineering** heat cards: Heat offered and Who could take it (the heat-pump COP in cards.js heatEcon and bestHeat, and the sink temperatures 60 / 70 °C), and the full tool's connection cards.
- **50–60 °C** (register heat_temp, liquid-cooled; tool/data/arch.json dcTemp, "the liquid figure is marked unverified (?)"): the **Architecture** side: Arrange's direct-heat test for each program (arch.js `lift`: direct / partly / heat pump), the Arrange heat gradient labels, and the Heat flow card's "hall heat temperature" (liquid case; the example lots run air-cooled, 25–35 °C).
- engine.js calc() (heat offered, homes) uses no temperature: COP 3–4.5 (R.cop).
