# Typical in-city data-centre size (19 Oct 2026) — for the residents' default size

All figures are OPERATOR CLAIMS; the power basis (IT vs total) is often not stated. Directory sites (Baxtel, Data Center Map, Inflect, DC Hub) were not used as sources.

## Edmonton (in city)
- Qu EDM1, downtown "Financial Core": "2.5 MW capacity"; building 18,000 ft², raised floor 15,712 ft² — qudatacentres.com/locations/edmonton-data-centre
- Qu EDM2, Edmonton: "2.5 MW Tier II | 3 MW Tier III"; building 35,000 ft² — same page
- Rogers Edmonton: 2013 expansion of about 6,000 ft² raised floor; no power figure — about.rogers.com (20 Nov 2013)

## Calgary (same province, market and regulator; in city)
- Arrow, 2712 37th Ave NE: "2 MW" utility capacity — arrowcalgary.ca
- eStruxture CAL-1, 7007 54th St SE: 9 MW total (tool precedents, operator claim); the operator's page now gives no figure
- Cologix CGY1: 0.4 MW (tool precedents, operator claim)
- DataHiveOne, 840 7th Ave SW (downtown): "70 KW of available power" — available, not total (RENX, 22 Oct 2025, trade press)
- Outside the city (not counted): eStruxture CAL-2 (Rocky View County) 26 MW; CAL-3 (Rocky View County) "90MW"

## Winnipeg, Saskatoon (prairie cities of similar character)
- Equinix WI1, 1450 Waverley St, Winnipeg: 2,323 m² colocation; no MW stated
- SaskTel Tier III, Saskatoon (2017): 24,000 ft² building, 9,650 ft² data hall; no MW stated

## Vancouver (in city, from the tool's precedents)
- 0.33 (Hut 8 VAN1), 0.69 (Hut 8 Vault), 1.6 (Cologix VAN5), 2 (VAN2), 2.14 (eStruxture VAN-2), 2.4 (VAN-1), 4 (Cologix VAN4), 5 (VAN3), 8 (Spencer Building) MW. Not counted: VAN-3 Burnaby 10 MW; 111 E 5th proposal 40 MW.

## Result
- Vancouver in-city: 9 sites, 0.3–8 MW, median 2.1 MW.
- Edmonton + Calgary in-city: 5 sites, 0.4–9 MW, median 2.5 MW (Edmonton alone: 2.5 and 2.5–3).
- Winnipeg and Saskatoon publish floor area but not power, so they add no MW figure.
