# Electricity and water to the same standard as heat (19 Oct 2026)

J.B.: water and electricity should be equals with heat. Each exchange now answers the same rows on its card: how much · who is nearby · what it is worth · what it costs (build, upkeep, life) · cost per unit over its life against worth · years to pay back · best set-up found · rules. Same method and 5.6% real cost of capital as heat.

Research: audit/research_2026-10-19/G_electricity_exchange.md, H_water_exchange.md (primary sources; gaps listed there).

## Electricity (Electricity card, "What it is worth here")
- Solar on the roof: cost per MWh (NRCan/IEA PVPS Canada 2024 capex by size; NREL O&M, 30 yr) against the energy charge it avoids (BC LGS 6.79 ¢; Alberta pool 2025 + EPCOR per-kWh). Best set-up: plant position (roof, yard, split) for most free roof. Clark Dr: $145–206/MWh against $68; Edmonton lot $118–194 against $50.
- A battery for the grid: cost per kW-year (NREL 2025 to PNNL 2022; 2.5–4% O&M; 15 yr) against BC Hydro DR $50/kW-season (with the Energy Storage Incentive's 80% shown) or AESO reserve averages if held every hour (upper bound). Vancouver $234–352 ($84–151 after the incentive) against $50; Edmonton $234–352 against up to $99–383.
- EV charging: BC Hydro L2 28.28 ¢ less 6.79 ¢ → a port repays $15,000 after about 70,000 kWh (utilisation not published). Alberta: no public rate found.
- Opportunity money gate for batteries now reads this (yes only if the middle cost is below the lower worth).

## Water (Water card)
- Water the cooling buys: make-up × local water + sewer rate, for each cooling choice.
- Rainwater: tank cost per m³ used (Vancouver toolkit 2014 to US EPA 2013; 15–30 yr) against water + sewer. Clark Dr with towers: $4.96–31.69/m³ against $4.26–4.62. Best set-up: the cooling choice that uses most rain.
- Reclaimed water: no price published in either region; Quincy WA precedent.
- Who is nearby: cooling towers with City permits within 500 m (counts and distances only); EV stations within 1 km.
- Opportunity money gate for rainwater now reads this.
