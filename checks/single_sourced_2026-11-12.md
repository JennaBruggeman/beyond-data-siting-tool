# Register quantities on the cards with thin support (12 Nov 2026, read-only)

For the main chat. A quantity is listed when a card uses it and it has only one source, or confidence 50 or below, or its formula or coefficient says "our reading" or "our arithmetic". Sorted by how many cards use it.

## Weaknesses first
- "Used by a card" means the card's logic link lights the part (BD.f1logic: cardIds and per-figure ids) or the card's own code or source lines quote the part's id (checks/tests/single_src_scan.js). A card that reaches a figure through the engine without naming its id is not counted, so the card counts are a floor.
- 138 of 225 quantities are named by no card this way; they are not assessed here.
- "Figure" is the coefficient text, shortened to 150 characters; the full text is in register/formula_register.json.
- Confidence is the register's own rating (CLAUDE.md §3); the "our reading" / "our arithmetic" test is a text search, so it also catches a part where only one input is our reading.
