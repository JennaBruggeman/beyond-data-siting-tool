# J.B.'s 4 Nov 2026 uploads — register pass

Read by Claude from the uploaded PDFs (pdftotext; page = PDF page unless "printed"). Rules as BRIEF_v2.md. "?" = not in the text. "our reading" marked. Uploads are not committed.

Short answer:
- **Public edge (Edmonton):** the Winter Design Guidelines give no canopy, plaza or seat dimensions. Their only numbers are a 1:1 street wall to road width, shadow studies at 0900/1200/1500 MST, and microclimate review over six storeys. The bylaw sets the hard numbers: wind statement at ≥20 m height and CFD study at ≥40 m; Pathways ≥1.8 m; weather-protected main entrances in MU and CB, but **none in BE, IM or IH**; non-residential Ground Floor ≥4.0 m in MU.
- **Loading (Vancouver s. 5):** Class A 5.5 × 2.7 × 2.3 m; Class B 10.2 × 3.4 × 3.8 m (3.5 m for a single lane-access space); Class C 23.1 × 3.6 × 4.3 m with a 14.0 m aisle; loading ≥4.6 m from any residential window or vent.
- **Loading (Edmonton s. 5.80):** confirmed. One 9.0 × 3.0 × 4.0 m space is needed from 2,500 m² and two above 7,500 m². Outdoor loading is screened by a Landscape Buffer ≥1.8 m high.
- **Heights:** the 5.70 roof exemptions are confirmed. A parkade's ground floor is ≥4.0 m (5.80 4.2.7). A Storey is measured top of floor to top of floor; a Basement counts as a Storey when the floor above is >1.83 m above Grade.
- **Existing quotes:** the Bylaw 20001 upload is byte-identical to the local copy (as adopted, PDF 3 Jan 2024), so later amendments are still not checked. All arch_zon_* and bylaw-quoting edm* quotes were re-found, with three small corrections (see the end).
