# What the massing contains: public programs (group C, 2 Nov 2026)

Brief: BRIEF_support.md, group C. Rules as BRIEF.md. 1 sq ft = 0.0929 m². "Our arithmetic" = ours, not the source's.

## Headlines
- **Two kinds of grossing factor. Don't mix them.**
  - Eastglen (Edmonton) 1.3–1.5: this already **includes washrooms, mechanical and electrical**.
  - ARUPLO library +25 %: also includes public washrooms, storage, garbage and mechanical.
  - Calgary component factors 0.98–1.45, mostly 1.05–1.20 (facility 1.12–1.19): these **exclude** support spaces, which are listed as their own line (factor 0.98–1.11).
- **Library: the current 930 m² holds.** The ARUPLO sample branch grosses to 466–928 m², and VPL's ~10,000 sq ft matches the top end.
- **Community room (new variant): ≈285–330 m² gross**, built around one 139 m² room for 30–80 people (Eastglen).
- **Gym: ≈404–467 m² gross**, against 372 m² now. The current 372 m² is Trout Lake's fitness floor only, with no support or circulation.
- **EV hub: ≈150 m² for a small working hub** (4 chargers, 6 stalls). This excludes the utility transformer zone (?).
- **Winter garden and covered plaza: still no sourced main-space size (?).** Only the support rules can be set.
