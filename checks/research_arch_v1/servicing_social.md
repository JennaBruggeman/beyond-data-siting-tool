### A8. Fire department access per program

Already sourced (security_rest.md s. 1; adj_service.md s. 7): principal entrance 3–15 m from the access route (3.2.5.5.(1)); ≤45 m to the FDC or entrance; **each cut-off portion its own entrance ≤45 m** (3.2.5.5.(4)).
