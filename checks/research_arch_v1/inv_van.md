## Per-facility notes (short)

- **Harbour Centre, 555 W Hastings (rows 1–4):** one tower holding four operators. A P1 chiller room (BP-2019-01298) and a BC Hydro primary-service upgrade (DB-2022-01818) are the plant traces in the permits. All permits carry the By-law 6555 note: air-conditioning ≤55 dB day / 45 dB night (sources/11).
- **VAN1 capacity:** the web page gives 520 kW and the Apr 2026 sheet gives 400 kW. Keep both, labelled.
- **VAN5 "6.23 MW":** the scope is unclear. It may be the whole building, so don't map it as VAN5's own figure.
- **VAN4 MW:** the page said 4 MW on 29–30 Oct and 6.5 MW on 2 Nov. The 2021 release says 4 MW and the 2026 brochure says "adds an additional 3 MW". This open item for J.B. stays open.
- **Spencer Building:** the permits don't name an operator. The JLL brochure (broker claim) names SBCH as operator and Polaris Realty as owner, and gives the 5 MW. It is marketed for lease. Whether it is running now is not confirmed.
- **Hut 8:** the operator's own pages swap the addresses and the VAN numbers (the colocation page calls the 0.6 MW site VAN1). The 2018 TeraGo basement permit at 900 W Hastings fits "Vault at 900 W Hastings" (our reading).
- **3448 Cornett (row 15):** next to VAN3. The City page names WZMH as applicant but no operator. Lead only, not evidence: Baxtel calls it "Cologix VAN6" and says under construction; Cologix's own pages and brochure list no VAN6.
- **3438 Lougheed (row 16):** the City record shows only a change of use and an approval. Lead only, not evidence: Daily Hive names True North Sustainable Infrastructure, a closed loop, rooftop coolers and a heat room. None of it is in a primary or operator source yet.
- **M3 (row 17):** TELUS says it opens end 2026, but the City's rezoning is not decided. The 23 Jul 2026 hearing was pulled; the only Q&A was 28 Jan–10 Feb 2026. A building year (1977) appears in news only (lead). Ask whether TELUS can open under existing I-1A uses ?. The ground-floor restaurant and "active, high-quality public realm interface" (vanRR1_2026) point to a street-facing building, not a fenced one.
- **150 W Georgia (row 18):** the approved zoning permits data storage "throughout the building". The building form is unknown, so the type is provisional. Leads only, not evidence: news gives a 39 MW BC Hydro figure (attributed to Allied) and a 10-storey data centre form; Allied's 18 Aug 2025 release does not mention this site.
- **TELUS Garden data centre (row 19):** this is the only Metro Vancouver data centre with a regulator-recorded heat export. Its address and current status still need confirming.
- **Burnaby (rows 20–23):** no Burnaby permit dataset was read. Status rests on operator pages and the 2020 rezoning report.
- **Not found:** no primary record of an operating, proposed or refused data centre in Richmond, Surrey, Delta, Langley, North Vancouver, New Westminster or Port Coquitlam this pass. One Surrey land-use minutes file (13 Jul 2026) was checked and had no mention. That is absence of evidence only.

## Type reasoning (our reading)

- **(a) tower:** rows 1–6, 8, 9, 11, 25, plus 18–19 provisionally. These are floors or suites inside buildings with other occupants, and their plant sits in shared parkades or on roof wells.
- **(b) smaller urban:** rows 7, 10, 12, 13, 16, 17, 21–24. Each fills a whole small building or a unit on the street line. No fence or gate shows in the permits or sheets (arch_per_cologixVan4Sheet: none listed).
  - Row 17 is large (40 MW) but sits in this group because it is a converted building with a required public frontage.
- **(c) larger urban, secure perimeter:** rows 14, 15 and 20.
  - Row 14 (VAN3): J.B. saw a fence and gate.
  - Row 20 (eStruxture VAN-3): 55,000 ft² converted warehouse on 83,000 ft² of land; the datasheet states a 10 ft perimeter fence (OC).
  - Row 15 (3448 Cornett): a new 4,620 m² industrial block next to VAN3. The perimeter is not stated.
