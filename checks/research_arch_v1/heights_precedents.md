# Floor-to-floor and clear heights, precedents and guides — research (4 Nov 2026)

Rules as BRIEF_v2.md and BRIEF_heights_precedents_2026-11-04.md. "?" = not found. "our arithmetic" / "our reading" marked. New ids in heights_precedents_sources.json (arch_hp_*). Built on heights_basement.md (arch_hb_*) and heights_conservatory_edge.md (arch_hce_*); their rows are not repeated, only cited where they carry a figure.

Read method: every new PDF was read through WebFetch summaries (direct download blocked by the session proxy; not circumvented). Quotes are as returned; page numbers as the summary gave them. Verify before final use.

Short answer: **a stated floor-to-floor was found for only three programs** (library, pool with diving, laundry plant wall height) plus a US hospital system. Most programs have clear heights only. One sourced services depth (Fairfax library, 1.83 m by our arithmetic) and one interstitial depth (VA, ≈2.4 m) let clear heights be turned into floor-to-floor (our arithmetic).

Reminder (from arch_hb_vanI3): Vancouver I-3 storeys are ≥6.0 m first floor, ≥4.0 m above, whatever the program. Edmonton BE has no floor-to-floor rule.

### Clear height → floor-to-floor (our arithmetic)

Floor-to-floor = clear + depth. Light depth 0.51 m (housing), institutional 1.83 m (Fairfax). Not valid for long-span roofs (pool, sports hall), where the roof structure, not a floor plenum, governs (depth ?).

| program | clear (sourced) | + 0.51 m | + 1.83 m | Van I-3 minimum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clinic | 2.4–2.7 | 2.9–3.2 | 4.2–4.5 | 4.0 above / 6.0 ground |
| gym fitness | 3.0–3.5 | 3.5–4.0 | 4.8–5.3 | 4.0 / 6.0 |
| community room | 3.3–3.6 | 3.8–4.1 | 5.1–5.4 | 4.0 / 6.0 |
| library | 4.27 | 4.8 | 6.1 (= Fairfax stated) | 6.0 ground |
| kitchen (proxy) | 3.0 | 3.5 | 4.8 | 4.0 / 6.0 |
| ETS room | 2.7 | 3.2 | 4.5 | below grade (s. 6.1) |
| climbing (UFC min) | 6.1 | 6.6 | 7.9 | double storey |
| sports hall | 6.0–8.5 | roof structure ? | roof structure ? | single storey, tall |
