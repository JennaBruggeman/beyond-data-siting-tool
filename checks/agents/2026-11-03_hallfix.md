# Hall placement fix (3 Nov 2026)

Branch `overnight-2026-10-30`. Not pushed. Brief: `HALLFIX_BRIEF_2026-11-03.md`.

## What changed

- **Hall height** (`site.js` `hallOptions`, `setHall`). With a ratio set, the hall is drawn at the storeys its floor needs. Storey heights come from the register: ground 6.0 m (`envelope`, `van_zdb_i2/i3`), hall floors 6.0 m (`burlingtonPlans2026`). The unsourced 5.5 m is not used. In I-2, I-3 and FC-2 the hall still stands on a ground storey kept for other uses.
- **Hall footprint options.** Hall storeys run from the most the height allows down to 1. Each footprint sits at the back of the buildable area: along the back or along a side, at 1, 1.5, 2, 3 or 4 : 1, or full width or length. The 6 m least side and the proportions are drawing rules (listed as unsourced).
- **Roof plant** sits on the hall's own roof (`plant.js`). The room-cooling density uses the hall's own storeys (`engine.js`).
- **Arrange** (`arch.js` `place`). It tries every option that passes `setupProblems` (plant fit, loading, fuel, noise) and places the programs on each. It keeps the option that draws the most m². Ties go to fewer programs spread over extra storeys, then street-entrance programs nearest the street, then option order. The result is deterministic and cached. If no option passes, the old full-width hall is drawn and flagged. The refuge-under-hall case keeps the full-width hall.
- **Programs** still try at grade beside the hall and on top of it (2 m grid, unchanged).
- **Not drawn → names the rule**, with its register id: height, coverage, street entrance, placement (at grade or not), keep-apart (found by testing again without the keep-apart widening), minimum sides, or no room left at grade. BE cap, FSR, zoning and share keep their flags.
- **Two fixes, only with a ratio set:**
  - The street front is the first row of cells inside the lot. On CLARK, row 0 falls outside the lot, so no street-entrance program could ever be drawn.
  - A street-entrance program takes the narrowest front that holds it.
- `arch_run.js`: `SPLIT=60/30` sets the split. The output now includes `hallPick`, `hallUV` and `hallStoreys`.
