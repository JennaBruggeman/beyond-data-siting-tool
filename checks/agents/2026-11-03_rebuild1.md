## 3. Ratios

- Design tabs: Ratios, Arrange (Programs + Arrange), Exchange (off until built), Compare.
- Coverage: slider up to the area inside the yards. Limit "?" except I-2 ("none").
- Allowed floor = min(lot × FSR, footprint × storeys under the height limit).
- DC share → MW range = DC floor ÷ (293.4–635.1 m²/MW: hall + UPS + Li-ion). No midpoint.
- Arrange tests one end, user's pick; default **low end**. That MW goes to `BD.S.answers.mw`; the DC floor to `answers.dcFloor`.
- The DC share is capped by: hall off the ground floor (Vancouver I-2/I-3/FC-2), the hall's room after loading and plant yard, and the largest MW every hard rule allows (`fitMax`).
- Start: the set-up's own MW at the low end; the rest to programs.
- Arrange: one split line with "Edit ratios". The space budget is the programs' share. A program past the share, or past the coverage at grade, is not drawn and named.
- Hard rules unchanged: height, FSR, BE caps, zoning, I-2/I-3 1.00 non-industrial.
- `site.js`: with a ratio set, the hall is sized to the DC floor and drawn full width at the back of the buildable area (a drawing choice, not a rule), so the street front stays free. No effect without a ratio.
