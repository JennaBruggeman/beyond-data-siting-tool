## Most serious five

1. **Uses that aren't permitted still get drawn** (arch.js:145–148). A use the zoning table says is not permitted, such as Community Service in Edmonton IM or IH, is placed and only outlined in red. It should be blocked, or offered only as a rezoning the user chooses.
2. **The Vancouver 1.00 FSR cap on non-industrial uses isn't checked** (arch.js:83). Public programs are capped only by the zone's total 3.0 FSR, so in I-2 and I-3 they can take about three times what the by-law allows. The source is checked and enough to code.
3. **Fire rules are notes only** (arch.js:151–154). Separations under NBC 3.1.3.1 are written as notes, not applied. A separate building is spaced at a drawn 3 m, not by NBC 3.2.3. Fire access under NBC 3.2.5 (access route, entrance 3–15 m or within 5 m in VBBL) is never tested.
4. **Hard keep-apart distances are flagged, not blocked, or not tested at all** (arch.js:60, 64, 155–157):
   - Evaporative plant at 7.6 m or 3 m (NBC 6.3.2.15) is flagged only.
   - Roof generators at 3 m (NBC 6.3.2.9) are flagged only.
   - Intake and garbage distances (1.5, 3 and 4.5 m) aren't modelled.
5. **The one-suite rule and the street-entrance rule are notes only** (arch.js:151, 159). Under NBC 3.1.3.2.(2) a second residential use (refuge overnight plus overnight shelter) can share the F-2 building. Under Van ZDB 10.21.1 a use that needs its own street entrance can be placed off the front, or on the roof.

Also high, just outside the five: opps.js:18 rates aquaculture in Vancouver I-2 "possible with a change" although the live-animal ban rules it out. arch.js height and FSR checks run against the model's own values (M.H, a user-typed FSR), not the by-law's.
