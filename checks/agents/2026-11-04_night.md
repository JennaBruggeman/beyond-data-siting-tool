## Part 3: DC rooms as masses
New file `tool/dcrooms.js`. The rooms are sized from `dc_rooms.json`:
- hall: `arch_dc_hall`
- electrical (UPS) room: `arch_dc_electrical`
- battery (Li-ion) room: `arch_dc_battery`

**Sizes**
- Each room is drawn at one end of its m²-per-MW range, never at a midpoint.
- Architecture uses the end that matches the MW Ratios tests. The low end of the MW range pairs with the high m² per MW.
- Other templates use the high end.

**Placement (drawing choices, labelled as such)**
- The hall keeps the set-up's footprint and position.
- The electrical and battery rooms sit next to the hall on its first floor.
- The rest of the footprint is drawn pale as "Other rooms, size ?".
- Where the zone keeps the ground storey for other uses, that storey is drawn under the hall, so the hall does not float.

**Not drawn, listed with "?"**
- Transformers, portals, washrooms, MV switchgear, telecom, fire suppression, storage, security room, offices, break room, janitor.
- Heat recovery and the ETS are drawn only when there is a heat exchange.

**Views**
- Engineering 3D: a Massing / Equipment toggle, Massing by default.
- Architecture: 3D, explode, plan and section.
- **Not done:** the Massing / Equipment toggle in Architecture. The rack layout belongs to the Engineering hall.
- The report's own iso drawings still show one DC mass.
