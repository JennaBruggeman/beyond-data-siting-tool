# Why Ratios gives the data centre 6–7 % on E14715 and E4103 (4 Nov 2026)

Branch `overnight-2026-10-30`. Not pushed. No tool code changed.

## Answer

**A real rule: the noise by-law, with the default set-up.** Not a bug in the ratio search.

- Rule: 60 dBA night limit at the neighbour's property line (Bylaw 14600 s. 22(1); register `noise_limit`). The plant runs 24 h, so the night limit governs.
- Default set-up on both lots: air cooling inside, air-cooled chillers on the roof, screened (`plantAt roof`, `vis screen`, `inside air`).

## How the share is set (arch.js)

- `ratioInit` starts at the set-up's own MW. `dcCapPct` caps it at `fitMax` (no ratio) × 635 m²/MW (high end, so the low MW end) ÷ allowed floor.
- The set-up's MW comes from `engine.js` 250: the low end of the floor-only range, then `fitNear`. Both lots fail there, so `fitNear` drops to the largest passing size.

| lot | allowed floor | fitMax, default set-up | share | first size that fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E14715 | 16,139 m² | 1.8 MW | 1.8 × 635 / 16,139 = 7 % | 2.0 MW: 61.8 dBA at 14612 124 Ave NW (BE), 63 m |
| E4103 | 8,947 m² | 0.9 MW | 0.9 × 635 / 8,947 = 6 % | 1.0 MW: 60.7 dBA at 4143 97 St NW (IM), 26 m |
| E06B | 6,854 m² | 3.8 MW | 3.8 × 635 / 6,854 = 35 % | — |

The big lots have 2.4× and 1.3× E06B's allowed floor, but a lower noise-capped MW. So the share falls.

## Evidence (probe, our arithmetic)

- **E14715, 2.0 MW:** 4 chillers, 2 × 2 on the hall roof. Low-noise units (Trane RTAF 280 LN, maker claim) bring it to 61.8 dBA. The screen gets **no credit** toward the two lots on 124 Ave NW: the far pair of chillers is 20.4 m from the screen on that side, past the NSW 2021 condition of 20 m (`arch_nz_nswDcNoise`). At 1.8 MW (3 units in a row), the screen counts and the worst lot is 57.6 dBA.
- **E4103, 1.0 MW:** the screen counts (−8 dB). 3 chillers, 26 m from the IM lot line: 60.7 dBA with low-noise units and the screen. At 0.9 MW, 58.4 dBA.
- **E4103, 0.5 MW** fails a different rule: 1 of 2 chillers does not fit on the smaller roof. This is the known non-monotonic case (2026-11-04_bugfix.md); `fitNear` already handles it.

## Other set-ups lift the cap (fitMax, no ratio)

| set-up | E14715 | E4103 | E06B |
|---|---|---|---|
| default (roof, screen) | 1.8 | 0.9 | 3.8 |
| plant in the yard / split | none (noise) | none (noise) | none (noise) |
| plant indoors | 21.9 | 9.0 | 3.8 |
| liquid cooling (dry coolers) | 21.9 | 3.7 | 3.8 |

So Ratios is right for the set-up it was given. Choose "Bring it indoors" or liquid cooling and the share can rise.

## Seen along the way (not changed)

- **Screen ring sits 0.5 m inside the plant clearance, not outside it.** `engine.js` 468 (and the drawing, `scene3d.js` 178) call `BD.geo.offset(hull,0.5)`. On E14715 the hull came out 0.5 m smaller on every side. The sign of `offset` follows the ring's winding (`scene3d.js` 101 tests both ways for this reason). Pushing it outward would make the 20 m test fail by more (21.4 m), so it does not cause this cap. Worth a fix of its own; it will move noise figures in golden.
- The 20 m distance is measured from each unit's centre. Measuring from the unit's edge would be our reading (?).

# Fix (J.B. approved, 4 Nov 2026)

Three commits, one per part. Not pushed.

| part | commit | what |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 76e22b3 | The screen now sits 0.5 m outside the plant clearance. `BD.geo.offset` moves a ring inward for d > 0 on either winding (tested on both windings), so 0.5 became −0.5 in `engine.js` noiseCheck and in the `scene3d.js` drawing. |
| 1 | 6ed2431 | The fit search runs the noise ladder before it caps a size (`site.js` model). If the roof plant still breaks the night limit with low-noise chillers and a screen, the model is rebuilt with the plant in an enclosed plant room (NSW 2021, −25 dB, `arch_nz_nswDcNoise`; its floor comes out of the building, `plant.js`). That model is used if it passes, and the room is named as required. A block stays only when no measure passes. `fitMax`, `fitSizes` and `fitNear` get this through `setupProblems`. `fitPlan()` names the measure the largest size depends on. It shows in Ratios, on the set-up card ("Largest that fits" row; Cooling row), in the What fits headline and in the report row. `fitMax({asIs:true})` gives the size with the plant as set up. |
| 2 | b58b42a | Architecture mode defaults to the enclosed plant room while no visibility is chosen (`A.vis='indoors'`, `A.visDefault='arch'`). This is a default choice, not a rule. It is labelled "(Architecture default)" and is cleared when a visibility is chosen (`addons.js`). |
