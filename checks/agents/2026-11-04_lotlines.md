**Main findings**

- **Vancouver defines no front, rear or side property line (?).** It only defines the yards. It also gives:
  - corner sites: the front is the shortest street boundary (s. 10.26.1(b));
  - equal street boundaries: the Director of Planning decides;
  - a lane is "secondary access at the side or rear".
- **Edmonton defines all of them** (s. 8.20):
  - Corner Lot: the streets meet at less than 135°.
  - Front Lot Line: the shorter street line on a corner.
  - Rear Lot Line: the line "furthest from and opposite" the front.
  - Interior side: every other line.
  - Double fronting sites have 2 front lines (s. 5.130.4).
- **New rule found:** in I-2 sub-area B, I-3 and FC-2, the exterior side yard on a corner equals the front yard.
- **Not found (?):**
  - any number for "approximately parallel" or "opposite";
  - any rule for panhandle or notched lots;
  - which line is the front on a Vancouver single-lot double fronting site.

## 2. Build

**Code**
- `tool/lotside.js` `classify`: sorts each lot line into one of the classes, using the tests in lotlines.md.
- `tool/site.js`: gives each line its own yard.

**Classes**
- front;
- flank (flanking side);
- rear;
- side;
- unclear: the by-law does not settle it. The line takes the deepest yard it could have, and is flagged "which yard this edge has is not known (?)".

**Edmonton (BE, IM, IH, EIB, EIM)**
- The setback still depends on what each line abuts, but is now worked out per line.
- If a line abuts nothing on file, or a parcel with no zone on record, it takes the deepest setback in the table. It is flagged.

**Where the per-line yards apply**
- the buildable area and the hall (`inscribe` keeps clear of every line's yard);
- loading;
- the plant yard;
- the Architecture programs;
- the line of the yards in 3D;
- the fence line and the fence-in-yard check (bug 6).

**Swap the ends** still works as an override: it swaps front and rear.

**The setup card** now has a Lot lines table. For each line it shows the class, the reason, the yard and any flag.
