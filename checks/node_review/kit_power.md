## Notes

1. **Cat 3516C Level 1 / Level 2.** The extracted text placed the "Level 1" and "Level 2" labels unclearly around the two tables. The 5007 mm table is assigned to Level 2 by its position and by the cat.com enclosure page (125120), which prints "Height: Level 1: 166.6 in; Level 2: 197.1 in". Width: the table prints 2671 mm for both levels, but the footprint row says 8' 9" for Level 2 (about 2667 mm). Treat the Level 2 width as unresolved. The tool's 13.9 × 2.7 × 5.0 m matches the Level 2 printed values.
2. **Radiator airflow (3516C).** A horizontal radiator with 8 electric fans is printed. The sheet does not say whether air discharges up or out an end. I have not inferred it.
3. **~1 MW genset.** The tool's 8.6 × 2.5 × 3.7 m is not matched exactly. Cat C27/C32 sizes are 7.645 m long with a 1000–2000 gal tank, or 9.750 m with a 3600 gal tank. Width 2.554 m and heights of 3.2–3.8 m are close. The kW range covered by LEHE0407 is not printed. The Cummins F-1493 values are from 2008 and are older catalogue data.
4. **Cummins S-1443 / C2000 D6.** cummins.com product pages returned fetch errors. Cummins PDFs found only on dealer sites (csdieselgenerators, woodstockpower, ninexpower) were not used, per the rules. The one Cummins sheet read via a dealer host (NAS-6746, C1750/C2000D6E open set, 5708 × 2470 × 2441 mm) is an open set without enclosure and is excluded from the table.
5. **Transformer letters.** For Eaton CA202003EN and PEAK CA202002EN, letters A–I are defined only in Figure 2, which could not be seen. I = 24 matches the printed "24-inch-deep cabinet", but that is an inference. Eaton CA022021EN columns HTX / WTX / DTX are read as transformer height / width / depth; the text does not spell them out.
6. **Blocked or unreadable.** Direct downloads by curl were blocked (403) for cat.com and eaton.com. Siemens support.industry.siemens.com returned an error (the cache.industry mirror worked). The Galaxy VX ESS dimensions page URL was too long to fetch. The Schneider 6037CT9901 PDF link could not be resolved from its download page. No logins or paywalls were bypassed.
7. **Manufacturer claims.** All values are catalogue / reference values. Cat and Eaton print "not for installation design" or "for estimating purposes only". Sound levels (dBA) and run time are manufacturer claims.
