## Notes

1. **Racks.** Schneider and Vertiv give overall sizes on catalogue pages. The VR3300 drawing text was garbled when extracted, so its exact overall depth with doors is unconfirmed. The VR3307 page gives two depths (1100 and 1200 mm) and does not say which is right; the submittal drawing would settle it. For Rittal, only 1000 mm deep 42U variants were confirmed. No 1200 mm deep Rittal VX IT and no 48U VX IT were checked.
2. **Standards are paywalled.** EIA-310, IEC 60297-3-100, TIA-942, BICSI 002 and EN 15698-1 cannot be read in full. The free IEC 60297-3-100 Ed.1.0 (2008-11) preview shows table titles only, no values. The EN 15698-1:2025 preview lists "Table 1 Casing diameters" but no values. Copies on Scribd and pdfcoffee were not used.
3. **Aisle widths.** No standards-body figure could be read. The 1.2 m and 1.8 m figures are what makers build containment systems for. They are not code minimums. No hot-aisle width was found.
4. **CRAH.** Vertiv PWM figures are labelled Length × Width × Height; the flyer does not use the word "depth". The flyer lists Smart Coil conditions (36 °C / 30 % RH return, 18–26 °C water) but does not tie them explicitly to the capacity figures. PH capacities are net sensible, single circuit.
5. **Heat pumps.** The Sabroe dimension header prints "l L H". The order (length, width, height) was inferred from the values, so treat it as unconfirmed. The source is a maker catalogue section with no date or cover page, hosted on an event-platform CDN rather than a JCI site. Sabroe's own PUBL-7144 (2023) carries no readable dimensions. The 60→70 °C / 39→34 °C HeatPAC rating suits data-centre waste heat. Units over 1 MW are 5–6 m long skids. No dimensions were found for Friotherm, Carrier, Trane, MAN or Danfoss.
6. **Plate heat exchangers.** Leaflets do not print MW duty; duty depends on plate count and temperatures. So "1–5 MW" cannot be tied to a frame size from these documents. The DN200 ports on the T20 are only a hint at flow scale. The T35 and SWEP B649 dimension labels could not be mapped to H/W/L from the extracted text; check the drawings directly.
7. **Buffer tanks.** No heat-pump-specific design guide in l/kW was found for a 1–3 MW water-to-water unit. The Daikin figures are minimum loop water contents for stable compressor control, not thermal storage, and come from a chiller (≤ 50 °C) and a small air-to-water heat pump. The largest single Reflex tank is 5000 l. At, say, 6.6 l/kW, 1–3 MW would need several such tanks or a site-built tank; that sizing is an illustration, not a sourced value.
8. **Twin pipe.** Logstor's current catalogue PDFs on logstor.com (media/7241 TwinPipes design manual; media/7319 product catalogue) returned no readable text, so the 2014 catalogue (third-party copy) is used. Check it against the current edition. An isoplus statement that twin pipe goes to DN 250 (Series 1 and 2 only) appeared only on a directory site (environmental-expert.com), so it was not used.
