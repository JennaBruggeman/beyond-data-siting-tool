## What the permit record gives the register
- `cooling_type` and heat-rejection location per site, from the public record (rooftop condensers at 1050 W Pender and 175 W Cordova; rooftop chiller at 55 E 7th; P1 chiller room at 555 W Hastings; L8 roof well at 515 W Hastings) — confidence 90 for the fact of the equipment, no capacities.
- `it_load_claim`: no MW anywhere in permit descriptions; the only load traces are the BC Hydro primary-service upgrade (555 W Hastings, 2022) and the BC Hydro room (175 W Cordova, 2022).
- `build_cost` local datum: $28.5 M for a 4-storey conversion (175 W Cordova) and $18 M for three tower floors (515 W Hastings) — need gross floor areas from the BCA extract or footprints × storeys to express per m².
- Noise: every field-review permit carries the standard note that mechanical equipment must conform to By-law 6555 and that air-conditioning units are not to exceed 55 dB daytime / 45 dB night — the City applies the by-law limits at permit stage.
