## Implication
Vancouver: condition cannot be stated as MW headroom; "post-2031/32 substation capacity, subject to BC Hydro study and Call selection" (heat recovery scored only inside a 10% bucket). Edmonton: 1,200 MW (≈ city load) taken by two projects; ~20 GW queued; LTP models no DC load; Phase 2A favours BYOG; Woodcroft shows ~10 MVA N-1 headroom today and deficit by 2032 before any large load.
