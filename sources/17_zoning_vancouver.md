## 2. Use classification

**The by-law is not silent.** Section 2 of the Zoning and Development By-law (Definitions, June 2026 consolidation) defines a specific use, listed under Transportation and Storage Uses (category T):

> **Bulk Data Storage**: "The use of a wholly enclosed building for the storage of information on operating data servers."

This definition was added by the False Creek Flats amendments of 2017. The draft by-law for public hearing on 19 Sep 2017 adds it to the Transportation and Storage Uses in Section 2 as "the use of a wholly enclosed building, for the storage of information on operating data servers".

City staff use this classification for data centres. The CD-1 rezoning referral report for 111 E 5th Ave / 2060 Quebec St (RTS 19744, dated 30 Jun 2026, for Council on 14 Jul 2026) describes the data centre as falling in the "Transportation and Storage use" category and proposes adding bulk data storage through CD-1.

Related definitions (Section 2, June 2026) that a tool must *not* confuse with Bulk Data Storage:

- **Digital Entertainment and Information Communication Technology** is not a stand-alone use. It is folded into General Office ("any office use, including Digital Entertainment and Information Communication Technology"). The definition includes "data security, data storage, management and processing" and "telecommunications infrastructure, for hosting, storing and processing digital media, information and applications."
  - **Interpretation, needs City confirmation:** an office tenant's ancillary server room plausibly falls under General Office. A building whose principal use is operating data servers falls under Bulk Data Storage, which is how staff classified it in RTS 19744. The tool should not treat General Office permission as permission for a data centre.
- **Information Communication Technology Manufacturing** (category M) covers the production of electronic and communications equipment. It is not the operation of servers.
- **Storage Warehouse** (category T) covers "storage of goods, material, machinery, or equipment". It is a different use.
- **Utility and Communication Uses** (category U) are "Public Utility; Radiocommunication Station; Recycling Depot; Waste Disposal Facility." Public Utility is not separately defined in Section 2.

## 3. Per-district notes

- **I-2 (June 2026).**
  - Height cross-reference: s. 3.1.2.6 refers to "section 3.1.1.2", which does not appear in the extracted text. The only FSR increase found is s. 3.2.1.2, which applies to sub-area B. Flag for City clarification.
  - Sub-area A (s. 3.1.2.4) requires floor-to-floor heights of at least 6.1 m for the first floor and 4.0 m for upper floors, for buildings not existing on 31 Oct 2017. This matters for stacking data halls.
  - Sub-area B is within the False Creek Flats (Prior St / Clark Dr / Great Northern Way / Main St), shown on Map 1. Boundaries need GIS.
  - Office (general and health care) is capped at the greater of 235 m² or 33.3% of GFA.
- **Mechanical and electrical floor area (I-2, I-1C, I-3 draft, FC-2 draft).** The exclusion covers floors used for "heating and mechanical equipment" only at or below base surface, or in a rear-yard accessory building (I-2 s. 4.1.2; I-1C s. 4.1.2(c); I-3 draft s. 4.2.2(c); FC-2 draft s. 4.2.2(c)). No schedule mentions electrical equipment. Above-grade plant therefore counts toward FSR (interpretation of the text; confirm). The draft CD-1 by-law in RTS 19744 goes further: "Below-grade bulk data storage use and mechanical/electrical space for bulk data storage use is not excluded from FSR". It also notes the project needed an FSR of roughly 4.2 against the 3.0 allowed. This is a strong signal that City practice counts data-centre M/E space as floor area.
- **Conditional approval** (I-2 s. 2.2.1 and similar). The Director of Planning or the Development Permit Board must consider the schedule's intent and Council policies. The result is discretionary, so the tool should present it as "possible, discretionary" rather than "allowed".
- **Parking By-law 6059 (Section 4, June 2026).**
  - "No parking spaces are required for the off-street parking of motor vehicles accessory to any development in the City" (s. 4.1.1), except accessible spaces (s. 4.1.4(b)) and visitor spaces.
  - Maximums apply under s. 4.2. In I-3 the maximum is 1 space per 42 m² GFA. Downtown/Broadway: 1 per 115 m² (s. 4.2.5).
  - Note: the parking.htm index page still says "amended up to October 5, 2010", while the section PDF footers say June 2026. Cite the section PDFs.
- **Loading (Section 5, May 2026).** Bulk data storage is not named. Section 5.2.5 covers Transportation and Storage and Utility uses:
  - Class A: no requirement.
  - Class B: 1 space for the first 465 m² GFA, plus 1 for any portion of the next 1,860 m², plus 1 for each further 2,325 m².
  - Class C: 1 space for 2,000–5,000 m² and 2 spaces above 5,000 m² — the extract ties this wording to storage warehouse uses, so whether it applies to bulk data storage is UNVERIFIED.
  - Whether s. 5.2.5 applies to bulk data storage or the Director decides under s. 5.1.4 (similar use) is UNVERIFIED.
  - Space dimensions (s. 5.5.1): Class B 10.2 × 3.4 × 3.8 m; Class C 23.1 × 3.6 × 4.3 m.
- **Reference table conflict.** In the City's *Land Uses by Zoning District* (June 2026; "for reference only and does not form part of the Zoning and Development By-law"), the extracted Bulk Data Storage row (FC-2, I-1C, I-2, I-3*) read as if it sat in the Outright column. The I-2 and I-1C schedules both say **Conditional**. The schedules govern; the table was probably misread by text extraction.

## 4. CD-1 handling

**Facts (SOURCED):**
- The CD-1 District Schedule (Nov 2022) says the permitted uses are those "listed in the by-law that designates" each CD-1 district. Sections 2–13 of the schedule apply unless that by-law says otherwise.
- The City's CD-1 index (cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca) lists by-laws only once "enacted by City Council". Range headers run to "926-1000" and the index links to CD-1 (935). Several entries are repealed, e.g. CD-1 (88), repealed 29 Jul 2026.

**Not established:**
- The number of active CD-1 districts and the number of CD-1 parcels: **UNVERIFIED**. Count them from the parcel dataset rather than citing a number.

**Why CD-1 parcels cannot be computed generically:** each district's uses, FSR, height and yards are written in its own by-law (and often in amendments), in free text.

**PROPOSAL for the tool:**
1. If `ZONING_DISTRICT` is a CD-1, show the status "Site-specific (CD-1 by-law No. X)". Link the by-law PDF and do not compute an envelope.
2. Keep a small hand-coded lookup only for CD-1s known to permit Bulk Data Storage, such as CD-1 (818). Mark each entry "SOURCED from by-law [number, date]".
3. In trade-off mode, treat every other CD-1 parcel, and any parcel in a district where the use is not permitted, as a rezoning pathway. RTS 19744 is the calibration case: I-1A to CD-1, 40 MW, waste-heat delivery to the NEU made a "material condition" of the use. The tool should show that the rezoning route gives more reciprocity but involves process, time and risk.
